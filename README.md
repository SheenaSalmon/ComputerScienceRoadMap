# Computer Scientist Roadmap

A curated roadmap for learning the core ideas and practical skills of computer science. Use it to organize your studies, find areas to explore, and build a learning path that fits your goals.

## Getting started

1. Choose a programming language and learn its fundamentals.
2. Strengthen your math foundations, especially discrete mathematics and logic.
3. Study data structures and algorithms, and practice implementing them.
4. Explore core systems topics, then choose a specialization that interests you.
5. Apply what you learn through projects and regular practice.

## Topics to explore

- **Programming:** fundamentals, paradigms, and programming language concepts
- **Theory:** discrete math, logic, algorithms, and theory of computation
- **Systems:** computer architecture, operating systems, and networking
- **Data:** databases, information management, and distributed systems
- **Software development:** design, testing, version control, and collaboration
- **Specializations:** AI and machine learning, cybersecurity, graphics, and human-computer interaction

## Make the most of the roadmap

Learn actively: write code, solve problems, explain concepts in your own words, and build projects. Revisit topics when needed. This is a flexible guide rather than a fixed curriculum; adapt it to your background and objectives.
